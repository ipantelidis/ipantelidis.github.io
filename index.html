MSc AI student at the University of Amsterdam

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Amsterdam with a passion for research. My thesis, in the VIS Lab, studies world models that act as action-conditioned verifiers in video games. Alongside it I work on two research projects: one examines the physical understanding of video foundation models, the other studies generative reranking in information retrieval.

Before this I studied Computer Science at [BSC_INSTITUTION], where I graduated first in my class with a 9.27/10. My bachelor thesis, supervised by Ion Androutsopoulos and John Pavlopoulos, was on diagnostic captioning: generating diagnostic text from medical images.
